Profile & Vision

About Me

The driving passion behind hybrid propulsion, aerospace systems, and manufacturing innovation.

Engineering with Precision, Propulsion & Purpose

I am an Aerospace and Mechanical Engineer driven by the challenge of taking complex propulsion equations off the paper and onto the static test stand. During my undergraduate studies at IIUM, my obsession with rocket propulsion took flight when I tackled one of the most stubborn hurdles in hybrid rocketry: paraffin fuel grain brittleness.

By synthesizing additive manufacturing with propulsion chemistry — designing a 3D-printed PLA nested skeleton — our research achieved a 200% boost in structural integrity while improving combustion efficiency by 40%. That work sparked six peer-reviewed publications and led directly into captaining the university's sounding rocket team at TEKNOFEST and winning multiple Gold Medals at the Malaysia Rocket Competition.

Currently working as a Technical Engineer at Oldenburger Interior while leading the IIUM 2026 TEKNOFEST campaign, I combine hands-on mechanical fabrication, professional SolidWorks design (CSWP), and numerical simulation (ANSYS Fluent / Structural) to build aerospace systems that perform reliably under extreme loads.
